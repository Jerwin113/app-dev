## My Favorite Series: Loki

**Loki** is my favorite Marvel series. The story follows the God of Mischief after his escape with the Tesseract. His journey leads him to the *Time Variance Authority*, also called the `TVA`.

### Series Overview

| Detail | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Loki |
| Main actor | Tom Hiddleston |
| Genre | Science fiction, fantasy, and adventure |
| Setting | The TVA and different timelines |

### Main Characters

- **Loki**: The God of Mischief who faces the consequences of his choices.
- **Mobius**: A TVA agent who works with Loki.
- **Sylvie**: A Loki variant who values freedom.

### Why I Like Loki

1. The time-travel story keeps me interested.
2. Loki grows as a character and learns to care about others.
3. The series explores friendship, identity, and free will.

> My takeaway: Our choices shape who we become.
